Command desk quick guide

  1. Review urgency and the requested unit on each case.
  2. Select a case, choose its requested destination, then assign it.
  3. When a unit finishes, its room and team become available.
  4. Use pause to plan; speed changes the whole hospital clock.
  5. Keep flow, team energy, and community confidence healthy through shift end.

Welcome to the Harborlight command desk

You’re coordinating a busy shift, not diagnosing anyone. Every case is fictional. Prioritize the queue, assign the right operational destination, and keep limited teams and rooms turning over.

How a shift works

  1. The simulation runs in real time. The hospital clock covers an eight-hour day shift.
  2. New cases arrive in waves. Their priority, waiting time, and requested unit are visible.
  3. Select a waiting case, send it to its requested unit, and occupy one of that unit’s rooms and a team.
  4. After a short simulated service window, the case resolves and those resources return.
  5. Random operations alerts create tradeoffs. Your choices affect flow, team energy, and community confidence.

There is no real treatment guidance in this game. The scoring measures operations and coordination, not real-world clinical outcomes.
