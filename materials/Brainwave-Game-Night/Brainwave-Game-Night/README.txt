BRAINWAVE GAME NIGHT — OFFLINE EDITION

The game works without internet after download. No account is needed for this copy.

On a computer:
1. Unzip Brainwave-Game-Night.zip.
2. Double-click Brainwave-Game-Night.html to open it in your browser.

On Android:
1. Download the ZIP and open it in Files or My Files.
2. Tap Extract/Unzip.
3. Tap Brainwave-Game-Night.html and choose a browser (such as Chrome).
   If your phone opens a text preview, use Open with > Chrome.

Press Start to see each round introduction, then Begin timed round to start the clock.
Scores stay on the screen only while the game is open. Refreshing or closing it starts a new game.
All price-guessing amounts are fictional game prices.
