S3LFM@DE SOLUTIONS - PRACTICE ROADMAP

This library is for PRACTICE. It does not touch your live business website.

ROUND 1 - DESIGN REFRESH
Pick any Word sample. Change only fonts, colors, borders, spacing and alignment.
Save your version as V2.

ROUND 2 - REBUILD
Choose one sample and recreate it from a blank document without looking at the original until the end.

ROUND 3 - THREE STYLES
Make the same project Elegant, Classic, Modern, and Fun/Colorful.

ROUND 4 - HTML/CSS
Open an HTML_CSS_Practice folder.
Edit styles.css first. Refresh index.html in your browser after every few changes.
Then change the HTML structure.

ROUND 5 - CLIENT CHALLENGE
Pretend a client gave you the brief. Set a 45-minute timer and finish a usable first draft.

ROUND 6 - SELF REVIEW
Check: hierarchy, alignment, spacing, readability, consistency, contrast, spelling, and whether the design fits the audience.

Original

Your V2
Final
This lets you see your progress.
