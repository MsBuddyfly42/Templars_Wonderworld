TEMPLAR’S WONDERWORLD COMPLETE PERSONAL DOWNLOAD

Save and open on Windows

1. Download Templars-Wonderworld-Complete.zip.
2. Open File Explorer.
3. Click Downloads.
4. Right-click Templars-Wonderworld-Complete.zip.
5. Click Extract All.
6. Click Browse if you want to choose Documents as the destination.
7. Click Extract.
8. Open the extracted Templars-Wonderworld folder.
9. Double-click index.html.
10. Choose Chrome or Edge if Windows asks which program to use.

Checkpoint: Templar’s Wonderworld home opens. You can enter the Office
classrooms, play the Classroom Challenge, or open your reading shelves.

Keep the entire extracted folder together. Open index.html from the
extracted folder, not from inside the ZIP.

Included

224 resources: books, original classroom materials, practice files, puzzles,
learning activities, the six Honeybrook storybooks, and their combined ZIP.
The Classroom Challenge has 40 assignments and 800 questions.
The Office classrooms have 30 assignments with 120 multiple-choice questions
and 30 selectable strategy puzzles, plus the downloadable Word workbook.

Saving progress

This personal copy saves progress and scores in the browser on this computer.
It does not sync or import scores from the online Wonderworld account.
Use the same browser and keep the folder in the same location. Clearing
browser data can remove saved progress. Private browsing or some browser
settings can prevent saving; save failures are shown on the page.

The Classroom Challenge has Export scores backup and Import scores backup
under My report card. Save a backup before switching browsers or computers.

The portal and included learning activities can open without hosting or a
login. External links, optional web fonts, and externally linked media need
internet access. Some original project packages have their own setup steps.
No account credentials or private saved scores are included in this download.
